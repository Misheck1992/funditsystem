1. Introduction & Purpose of This Report

This report describes the capabilities, data processing activities, and operational scope of the Loan Management System (LMS) deployed by our organisation. It has been prepared in response to a request from the Data Protection Commission of Zambia (DPC) and is intended to assist the Commission in understanding how personal data is collected, processed, stored, and protected within this system.

The information herein is accurate as of the date of this report and reflects the current deployed state of the system.

2. System Overview

The Fundit Loan Management System is a web-based application built to support the operations of a microfinance/lending institution. It is hosted on the organisation's own infrastructure (on-premises server) and is accessed by authorised staff only via a secured internal network or VPN.

The system supports the following primary business functions:

  • Customer registration and Know Your Customer (KYC) onboarding
  • Loan application, appraisal, approval, and disbursement
  • Loan repayment scheduling and collections tracking
  • Fixed deposit account management
  • Collateral registration and monitoring
  • Staff and user account management
  • Financial and operational reporting
  • SMS and email communication to customers
  • System backup and data archiving

The system is multi-branch capable, meaning a single installation can service multiple organisational branches with separate data access controls per branch.

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

Personal data within this system is processed on the following lawful bases:

  • Contractual Necessity: Processing of customer financial and identity data is necessary to assess loan applications, execute loan agreements, and manage repayments.
  • Legal Obligation: KYC data (identity documents, beneficial ownership) is collected to comply with applicable Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and financial sector regulations.
  • Legitimate Interest: Communication via SMS and email for loan notifications, arrears reminders, and account updates.
  • Consent: Where applicable, customers consent to data collection and communication at the point of onboarding.

6. Access Controls & User Roles

Access to the system is strictly role-based. Each system user (employee) is assigned a role that determines which modules and data they can view or modify. No user has unrestricted access to all system functions without explicit administrative assignment.

RoleAccess Level
System Administrator Full system access including configuration, user management, and reporting
Loan Officer Customer registration, loan origination, KYC capture, collateral management
Cashier / Collections Officer Payment processing, repayment recording, transaction management
Branch Manager / Approver Loan approval, customer approval, reporting within assigned branch
Read-only / Reporting View reports and dashboards only; no data modification

Key access control features:

  • Each user logs in with a unique username and password (passwords are stored in hashed form).
  • Sessions expire on logout; concurrent login tracking is maintained.
  • All user actions are recorded in an activity log with timestamp, user identity, and action description.
  • Approval workflows require authorised approvers for sensitive operations (loan disbursement, customer activation).
  • Multi-branch support ensures branch staff can only access records within their assigned branch.

8. Data Retention

Customer and loan records are retained for the duration of the business relationship and thereafter in accordance with applicable financial sector regulations and the organisation's internal retention policy. The system supports:

  • Active Records: All current and historical loan records, repayments, and customer profiles are retained in the live database.
  • Closed Loans: Loan records are marked as "Closed" upon full repayment but remain in the system for audit and reporting purposes.
  • Deletion: Customer and employee records can be permanently deleted by an authorised administrator where required (e.g., data subject deletion requests).
  • Backups: Database backups are retained by the system administrator. Backup retention schedules are managed at the organisational level.
The organisation commits to defining and documenting a formal data retention schedule in line with the Zambia Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 requirements.

10. Reporting & Audit Capabilities

The system generates the following operational reports, all restricted to authorised users:

  • Loan Portfolio Report: Active, approved, pending, rejected, and closed loans by date range, branch, or officer.
  • Portfolio at Risk (PAR) Report: Loans in arrears summarised by loan officer.
  • Customer Reports: Customer demographics, approval status, and registration statistics filtered by date, gender, country, or officer.
  • Repayment / Collections Report: Payment history and outstanding balances.
  • Fixed Deposit Reports: Deposit account details, interest accrual, and maturity schedules.
  • Activity Audit Log: All system actions by user, timestamped.

11. Known Limitations & Areas Under Improvement

In the interest of transparency, the organisation acknowledges the following areas where data protection controls are being reviewed and strengthened:

  • Encryption at Rest: The database does not currently employ field-level encryption for stored personal data. This is under review.
  • HTTPS Enforcement: The system is currently deployed on an internal network. HTTPS/TLS will be enforced prior to any external-facing deployment.
  • Formal Retention Policy: A documented data retention and deletion schedule is being formalised.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication: MFA for system administrator accounts is being evaluated for implementation.
  • Data Breach Response Procedure: A formal incident response procedure for data breaches is being documented, including notification timelines to the DPC.

12. Declaration

The organisation confirms that personal data processed through this system is collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes and is not further processed in a manner incompatible with those purposes. The organisation is committed to full compliance with the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 of Zambia and will cooperate fully with the Data Protection Commission in all matters relating to data protection and privacy.
